bobby_actions: false

Report-only is the default because ad platforms turn small mistakes into invoices with admirable speed. Graduate Bobby by setting `daily_spend_cap` to a real currency amount, confirming `max_shift_pct`, and changing `bobby_actions` to `true` only after his reports have matched your own judgement for at least one week.
