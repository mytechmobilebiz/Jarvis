# Deviation and reconstruction log (0.6.2 option C rebuild)

Entries: `RECON-<n>` for reconstruction choices, `D-<n>` for deviations from the design. Append only.

## R1 groups (rebuild step R1, 2026-11-18)

**RECON-1 (J1-J9 numbering).** Sources name the lineage kinds of the closure (compression continuation with `_compressed_from` or a parent ending `compression`, branch, reset, delegate, rotation), J8 (copied history for conversations with no anchoring parent), J2 (`_compressed_from` on an orphan), the provenance join of an orphaned branch (`m18:j8_always_two`) and the reset exclusion (`m18:j8_reset_copies`), but not a numbered list. Chosen (groups.py docstring): J1 continuation by end_reason, J2 continuation by `_compressed_from` (also with a NULL parent), J3 branch, J4 gateway reset (both ways, as the step-1 report says "both link both ways"), J5 delegate (parent down only), J6 rotation with an inherited fork marker (continuation, both ways), J7 markerless child (O-D1, parent down only), J8 copied history, J9 provenance (orphaned branch with one copied row; a row carrying or absorbing a certain stone's uid: `shared_uid`).

**RECON-2 (source._markers order and two parents).** Round-3 fix A is followed as written (a fork marker counts only when it names the session's own parent; any with a NULL parent; then `_compressed_from`; then the parent's `branched`/`compression` end; a copied parent's own marker after a reopen reads `continuation`; a foreign marker keeps its kind). Not settled by the sources and chosen fail-closed: (a) the parent's end_reason is read before the copied-marker fallback (both give a both-ways link); (b) "two parents" (`SourceSession.conflict`, read by groups as `uncertain`) is set whenever any lineage marker names a conversation other than the parent read and is not the parent's own (copied) marker, also `_compressed_from` and also with a NULL parent and two different markers; (c) `copied_markers` lists the parent's own markers copied onto the child; they are not recorded as links (they name the grandparent, which the parent's own links cover).

**RECON-3 (J8 thresholds).** The sources show a "two" rule (`m18:j8_always_two`: an orphaned branch with ONE copied row joins by provenance) but not its exact scope, while i22's `later.joined_families` joins a family with one copied row. Chosen: an unanchored conversation with at least 2 distinct copy-position rows at the very role, call and time of another conversation's rows is a member (`copied_rows`); with 1 it joins as `uncertain` (held, never one-click) instead of not at all; an orphaned branch (branch lineage or `_branched_from`) joins as a member with 1 (`provenance`); a gateway reset with copied-looking rows joins only as `uncertain`; a match against a row that is itself a copy in another unanchored conversation (two copies of one origin) is weak evidence (`uncertain`). i22's one-row family join is kept unchanged alongside (Processor families are i22's families plus the groups).

**RECON-4 (uncertain).** Sources: "a parent that does not resolve, or conflicting parents, gives the uncertain state; uncertain rows are held, never one-click, and the export waits", and the mutant `gd1:uncertain_no_spread`. Chosen: a conversation is a member when a path of certain links reaches it and its own parentage is not in doubt; doubt is (a) two parents (backup `conflict`, the importer's `flags.two_parents`, or the backup, the archive and the recorded parent links naming different parents) or (b) joining as a child through a lineage link from a conversation that is not its current parent while that parent resolves nowhere here; everything else the group's links reach is `uncertain` (it spreads). Seeds (the withhold's own conversations) are always `withheld_here`. A recorded `uncertain` row never becomes `member` again (table trigger), and a recorded group never loses a conversation.

**RECON-5 (groups-paths N1/N2 read fail-closed).** The round-3 groups-paths skeptic found that an orphan (Hermes parent NULL) read as `delegated` through an inherited `_delegate_from` or Hermes v16's `'__orphaned__'` tag had copied-history matching switched off (confirmed leaks, not pending owner decisions; fix direction given in the report). Chosen: `Graph.unanchored` treats an orphan whose only parent reading is a delegate marker as unanchored, so J8 runs for it (resets stay excluded, as the skeptic's narrowed probe). Tests: `test_brain_groups_inherited_062.py::test_skc3_n1_*`, `test_skc3_n2_*`.

**RECON-6 (seeds).** "A pending hold never seeds a group" (`m18:seed_pending_too`). Chosen fail-closed: every certain stone of the withhold seeds its conversation, including a hold an admin kept (`review = 'kept'`) and a fold member.

**RECON-7 (schema and link kinds).** The option C schema goes into `0008_brain_withhold_identity.sql` (the matrix cites 0008 line numbers for the title trigger, R9 and the export gate; 0.6.2 is unreleased). New link kinds `delegate_from`, `reset_from`, `child` (the Hermes parent of a delegate, a reset or a markerless child) and `provenance`. i22's families (`later.session_families`, `identity.family_sessions`) keep reading only the row-copying kinds: widening them both ways would implement the parent side of O-D1 (pending groups-paths B/D).

**RECON-8 (titles).** The title trigger hides the title of every group conversation by membership alone, whatever the admin chose for `hide_title`; a title an admin's withhold hid outside the group is kept in `brain_hidden_titles` (append-only) and hidden by the same trigger; joining a group also blanks the title in the conversation's rollback copies.

**RECON-9 (R9 scope).** R9 blanks every message rollback copy of a group conversation (by the stored row it names, by the deleted row it keeps, or, for an update copy of a row later deleted, through that row's deletion copy), uncertain or member, also copies of rows an admin later released (fail-closed; R4's trust pairing may narrow this to releases covering the stored content).

**RECON-10 (export gate).** `brain_withhold_pending()` gains `group_sessions`, `uncertain_sessions`, `uncertain_waiting` and `group_rollback_copies_with_text`. `uncertain_waiting` counts rows of uncertain conversations that are readable without an exact `release` of that row for that group, or held with a pending stone. `decisions-export` refuses while it is not 0; restore's `--removals-only` export never refuses (as i22's unsettled gate).

**RECON-11 (uncertain rows held in R1).** The R1 task lists "uncertain ... (held, never one-click, export waits)", while the central sweep for every group row is R2. Chosen: R1 holds only the rows of uncertain conversations (`groups.hold_uncertain`: a pending stone `held with its conversation: an uncertain link to a withheld conversation`, relation `uncertain`), after a withhold, after every import run, at every repair (startup, import, rescan, repair) and after the legacy reset. Relation `uncertain` needs the typed word for a per-row release; the one-click release refuses a conversation an uncertain link holds; the holds list shows `uncertain` and never pre-selects such a row. The exemption check in `hold_uncertain` reads releases without trust pairing (the F1 pairing is R4).

**RECON-12 (decision backup).** A withhold (and Settle's scope) is decided on the newest backup that wrote the group's conversations whose link is certain (`gd1:definite_off`): the closure over the archive before the stones are written, uncertain conversations left out.

**RECON-13 (when the closure runs, and what it changes in i22).** Withhold: over the archive and recorded links. Import (writing run): over the backup, the archive and the links, recorded in `prepare_identity` before any row is written. Settle's scope: read only. Repair (start-up, every import, rescan, repair) and legacy reset: over the archive. Decisions files (version 5) carry `groups` and `hidden_titles` (ids only) and the new link kinds; `_carry_tables` adds them. The Processor treats a group conversation as a withheld family (later-backup classifier, Settle families, no key adoption).
