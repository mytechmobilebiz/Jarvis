## R1 groups (rebuild step R1, 2026-11-18)

**RECON-1 (J1-J9 numbering).** Sources name the lineage kinds of the closure (compression continuation with `_compressed_from` or a parent ending `compression`, branch, reset, delegate, rotation), J8 (copied history for conversations with no anchoring parent), J2 (`_compressed_from` on an orphan), the provenance join of an orphaned branch (`m18:j8_always_two`) and the reset exclusion (`m18:j8_reset_copies`), but not a numbered list. Chosen (groups.py docstring): J1 continuation by end_reason, J2 continuation by `_compressed_from` (also with a NULL parent), J3 branch, J4 gateway reset (both ways, as the step-1 report says "both link both ways"), J5 delegate (parent down only), J6 rotation with an inherited fork marker (continuation, both ways), J7 markerless child (O-D1, parent down only), J8 copied history, J9 provenance (orphaned branch with one copied row; a row carrying or absorbing a certain stone's uid: `shared_uid`).

**RECON-2 (source._markers order and two parents).** Round-3 fix A is followed as written (a fork marker counts only when it names the session's own parent; any with a NULL parent; then `_compressed_from`; then the parent's `branched`/`compression` end; a copied parent's own marker after a reopen reads `continuation`; a foreign marker keeps its kind). Not settled by the sources and chosen fail-closed: (a) the parent's end_reason is read before the copied-marker fallback (both give a both-ways link); (b) "two parents" (`SourceSession.conflict`, read by groups as `uncertain`) is set whenever any lineage marker names a conversation other than the parent read and is not the parent's own (copied) marker, also `_compressed_from` and also with a NULL parent and two different markers; (c) `copied_markers` lists the parent's own markers copied onto the child; they are not recorded as links (they name the grandparent, which the parent's own links cover).

**RECON-3 (J8 thresholds).** The sources show a "two" rule (`m18:j8_always_two`: an orphaned branch with ONE copied row joins by provenance) but not its exact scope, while i22's `later.joined_families` joins a family with one copied row. Chosen: an unanchored conversation with at least 2 distinct copy-position rows at the very role, call and time of another conversation's rows is a member (`copied_rows`); with 1 it joins as `uncertain` (held, never one-click) instead of not at all; an orphaned branch (branch lineage or `_branched_from`) joins as a member with 1 (`provenance`); a gateway reset with copied-looking rows joins only as `uncertain`; a match against a row that is itself a copy in another unanchored conversation (two copies of one origin) is weak evidence (`uncertain`). i22's one-row family join is kept unchanged alongside (Processor families are i22's families plus the groups).

**RECON-4 (uncertain).** Sources: "a parent that does not resolve, or conflicting parents, gives the uncertain state; uncertain rows are held, never one-click, and the export waits", and the mutant `gd1:uncertain_no_spread`. Chosen: a conversation is a member when a path of certain links reaches it and its own parentage is not in doubt; doubt is (a) two parents (backup `conflict`, the importer's `flags.two_parents`, or the backup, the archive and the recorded parent links naming different parents) or (b) joining as a child through a lineage link from a conversation that is not its current parent while that parent resolves nowhere here; everything else the group's links reach is `uncertain` (it spreads). Seeds (the withhold's own conversations) are always `withheld_here`. A recorded `uncertain` row never becomes `member` again (table trigger), and a recorded group never loses a conversation.

**RECON-5 (groups-paths N1/N2 read fail-closed).** The round-3 groups-paths skeptic found that an orphan (Hermes parent NULL) read as `delegated` through an inherited `_delegate_from` or Hermes v16's `'__orphaned__'` tag had copied-history matching switched off (confirmed leaks, not pending owner decisions; fix direction given in the report). Tests: `test_brain_groups_inherited_062.py::test_skc3_n1_*`, `test_skc3_n2_*`.

**RECON-6 (seeds).** "A pending hold never seeds a group" (`m18:seed_pending_too`). Chosen fail-closed: every certain stone of the withhold seeds its conversation, including a hold an admin kept (`review = 'kept'`) and a fold member.

## R3 derived (rebuild step R3, 2026-11-18)

**RECON-21 (fold and the 4,174 default-ignorable code points).** Sources: O-CI ("NFKC, casefold, Turkish dotted/dotless i, and removal of default-ignorable code points"), N5 (soft hyphen, zero-width characters), "4,174 default-ignorable code points", the skeptic's `ign_hangul_fillers` mutant. Every relation compares fflat (fold, whitespace collapsed); fwords (words only) for whole values and short texts. i22's exact-text membership rules (message_text, the same uid with the same text) are not folded: membership stays exact, relations (holds) are folded.

**RECON-22 (generic-v1).** Sources: O-D4 ("list generic-v1 and a 16-character minimum; a generic piece is neither demanded nor allowed as a carrier; anything longer is held"), test names on greetings and URLs. The list itself is not in the sources. Chosen (identity.generic, generic_phrase): a piece (a cut beginning, a whole value inside the withheld text) is generic when it has fewer than 16 characters (fflat) or its words are one of a fixed list of greetings, thanks and short replies (en, de, tr, fr, es, it) or it is a bare common URL (scheme, host, at most a search/index/wiki path). Reply quotes keep i22's own minimum (16 characters as JARVIS wrote them) and drop only the list's phrases, outside groups only (never a weaker rule than i22 inside a family).

**RECON-23 (the canonical decode and its caps).** Sources: "iterative decode with caps of 64 steps, depth 32 and a size budget; a capped row fails closed as not_checked" (N2), "20,000 JSON strings per text; texts over 2,000,000 characters", "W after the 20,000th JSON string is held through the whole-text unescape", the round-3 skeptic's F2 (the cap fires on ordinary nested reads: a round-4 point). Chosen (identity.canon): a work list of readings; a step is one decode operation (a JSON parse with its strings; otherwise the whole-text unescape and the literal read, the open literal included); cheap readings (quote markers, summary spans, prune values) cost no step; caps 64 steps, depth 32 nested decodes, 4,000,000 characters of readings, 2,000,000 characters per field (not decoded at all), 20,000 strings per document (the unescape still reads the rest). A cap, or a JSON parse that runs out of nesting (RecursionError), makes the field `not_checked`: held as a carrier whenever any withheld text is in hand, and never 'none' in a group (typed word needed). The oracle's cap is R5. Note: the importer itself already excludes a row nested past the JSON parser (invalid record: RecursionError), so that shape reaches the comparison only from other paths; the step cap is reached by a read listing more than about 64 JSON documents (F2's over-hold class, kept as f8d had it).

**RECON-24 (cut, part_of, contains over every field).** Sources: "row_relations/carrier_relations giving cut/part_of/contains over every field", D3's state names, the prune line "keeping W's first 40 characters is a cut", N1/N4, the M1 shapes, "Cuts under 32 characters are residual R5" (a skeptic's counting rule). Chosen: the relation codes `cut` and `not_checked` are added (TEXT_RELATIONS, CONFIRM_RELATIONS: the typed word is needed). The fields of a row are its content and its tool calls (row_fields; a backup row's raw tool calls: source_fields). A reading relates as identical (a whole value equal, also by words), contains (W of at least 16 characters inside), cut (a beginning of W of at least 16 characters, not generic: a whole value or a lead value that is a strict prefix of W, or the text before a cut mark, before the end of a value cut without a mark, or, in a truncated preview, before a line end, whose end is a beginning of W), part_of (a reply quote, i22's rule; in a family or group also a whole value inside W). The cut minimum is 16, not 32 (fail-closed). Middle pieces of W outside groups count only as reply quotes (N3/R5b are pending owner decisions; not implemented).

**RECON-25 (short W).** Sources: "word-sequence matching for short W (ambiguous not none)", 13546 ("a short W1, under 16 characters of its own words, quoted with '> ' markers"), `fqw` (">= 16 chars").
