JARVIS -- START HERE

MAC
  Use the Mac installer from your download page
  (the link in your purchase email). It works on
  Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or
  newer) with no security warnings. You do not
  need this folder.

WINDOWS
  Easiest: use the Windows installer from your
  download page. If Windows says "Windows
  protected your PC": click "More info", then
  "Run anyway". (The file is safe.)

  From this folder instead: double-click
  1-START-JARVIS-WINDOWS.bat

LINUX / SERVERS / DEVELOPERS
  Open a terminal in this folder and run:
  bash start.sh

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
  The first run from this folder downloads about
  250 MB and needs about 1 GB of free disk space.
  It usually takes 5-15 minutes. Then a setup
  screen opens and walks you through the rest
  (10-30 minutes). The installers skip the wait
  and open the setup screen right away.

STUCK?
  Email jarvis.agent.tech@pm.me and we'll get
  you running.
